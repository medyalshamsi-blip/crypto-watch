    Radar: early moves across Binance

    Fast moves on your watchlist: 15m pings

    Rule, fixed and replayed on 130 coins before it was added (27 Nov 2026): a completed 15m candle closes at least 0.75% above the previous close on at least 2× the average volume of the previous 20 candles; one ping per coin per hour. A ping means "look at the chart now", not "buy": in the replay (May–Aug 2026) 3.4% of pings were followed within 2 hours by a 5% hour against 0.5% at any moment, pings came first for 66% of those spikes (30 minutes ahead in the median), 97% led nowhere, and the average next hour after a ping was slightly negative. 5% hour: a 15m close 5% above the close one hour earlier, counted once per coin per day. Buyers, VWAP (00:00 UTC) and the 16-candle high are shown for context only. Pings appear on this page and as a pop-up message here; they are not sent to Telegram (about 1–2 per coin per day). Read-only.

    Breakout setups: 4H warning → 15m breakout → retest

    Rules, fixed before testing: the ceiling is the highest unbroken 4H swing high of the last 10 days (a swing counts once 2 lower 4H highs have closed after it). Near ceiling: price below it by at most 1 × ATR14 (4H). Early warning: also two rising 4H swing lows, Bollinger BandWidth (20, 2) in the lowest 20% of the previous 100 4H readings, and volume since 00:00 UTC at least 1.3× the same time of day over the previous 20 days. Breakout confirmed: a 15m close above the ceiling on 1.5× the average of the previous 20 bars. Paper entry: the first retest touches the level and a 15m candle closes back above it, above session VWAP (00:00 UTC); entry at the next open, stop 0.25 × ATR14 (15m) below the retest low, target +2R, exit after 24 hours at the latest, at least 2R to the first resistance. Costs per side: 0.10% Binance fee plus 0.05% slippage and spread, inside 1R. Only completed candles are used. Read-only: nothing is bought or sold, and no TradingView alert is set.

    Holdings and risk

    How it reads. Main read: close vs EMA200, EMA20 vs EMA50, MACD vs its signal and vs zero, RSI above 55 or below 45 (+3 or more Bullish, −3 or less Bearish). Playbook: the rules of your trading books, the same scanner as the Python version. WAIT when no key level is at price, or when R:R is below 1:3 on both sides. Triggers count on a daily close (00:00 UTC, 01:00 Berlin); an intraday touch only highlights the row. Odds come from a zero-drift block bootstrap (20,000 paths, volatility 0.6×RV30 + 0.4×RV90): they measure how far normal volatility can carry price, not where it will go.

    Read-only: no orders, no exchange keys, nothing sent to TradingView beyond reading data. Not financial advice.
